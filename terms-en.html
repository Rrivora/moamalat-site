1. The service

Shirkati is an Android app that helps a business owner record sales, installments, stock, cash boxes, suppliers and employees, and keeps them in the company's account on our servers with a copy on the device.

The app is a record-keeping tool, not a lender or financier

Shirkati is a ledger in which a merchant records what they sold to their customers in installments and what they collected. It does not provide loans or financing, does not collect or transfer money, and is not a party to any agreement between the merchant and their customers.
