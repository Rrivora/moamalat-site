Privacy Policy

Shirkati (شركتي) · Developer: Rivora Last updated: 28 September 2026 · Applies to version 2.0.0 and later

This is the English version of the Arabic policy. Both say the same thing; if they ever differ, the Arabic version applies.

In short

What you record in Shirkati — products, customers, invoices, installments, cash boxes, suppliers, employees and attached photos — is stored in your company's account on our servers at Supabase in Frankfurt, Germany, with a copy on your device. We keep it to provide the service: so the app works offline and then syncs, so you find your company as it was on any new phone, and to manage your license. We do not sell your data, do not share it with third parties, show no ads, and use no analytics or tracking tools.

This policy explains how the Shirkati Android app handles your data. It covers the app and this website.

2. Where to find this policy and deletion inside the app

In the app: Settings → “Privacy & account”, which contains:

  • “Privacy policy” — opens this page;
  • “Delete account and data” — opens the deletion page, with a “Request deletion by email” button below it that opens an email to us containing your company name and number.

The account and data deletion page also works in any browser, without installing the app or signing in.

3. How the app works

You sign in with Google and create a company with its name, phone, country and currency; a company code is generated automatically. After that:

  • everything you record is saved on your device immediately, so you can work offline;
  • when the device is online, what you recorded is uploaded to your company account on the server;
  • the server holds the master copy: your company data is downloaded from it when you sign in on a new phone, and it tells the app your license status.

Who can see your company's data

Server rules restrict reading and writing each company's data to its members. Data is encrypted in transit (HTTPS), but it is not encrypted on the server with a key only you hold, so we — as the service operator — can technically access it. We do so only to provide support you request, to fix a fault, or when required by law.

5. What we do not collect or do

  • We do not share your data with third parties and do not sell it. Supabase is a service provider that stores it on our behalf only.
  • No analytics, no advertising, no crash reports sent to anyone.
  • We do not read your contacts. When you pick a customer's number, Android's contact picker opens and the app receives only the number you choose.
  • No audio recordings, and no background location tracking.

6. Notifications

All notifications are generated on your device: reminders for due installments, alerts when an operation is rejected during sync or attachment storage is nearly full, and a prompt to confirm a new suggested US dollar rate. We use no external push service. To turn them all off, disable the app's notification permission in your phone settings.

8. External services

  • Supabase — the server provider that stores your company data and files, in Frankfurt, Germany.
  • Google Sign-In — we receive only your name, email and profile photo; the app requests no access to Drive or Gmail.
  • Google Play — to check for a newer version, directly between your device and the store.
  • OpenStreetMap and Esri maps — they receive your IP address and the area shown only, nothing about your customers.
  • WhatsApp and share targets — only when you choose to share an invoice, statement or reminder; that app's policy then applies.
  • Exchange rate — fetched by our server from open.er-api.com; your device never contacts it and nothing about you is sent.

9. Data security

  • All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted with HTTPS.
  • Your session key is kept in Android's secure keystore.
  • Server rules restrict each company's data to its members.
  • Android's automatic backup to Google is disabled on purpose, so an old copy is never restored without your knowledge.
  • No system is flawless; if we learn of an incident affecting your data, we will notify you.

10. Retention

  • Company data and files: on the server while the company account exists.
  • Records you delete in the app: move to “Deleted” so they can be restored, and are erased permanently with the company account or on your request.
  • The copy on your device: removed when you uninstall the app — this does not delete your company account on the server.
  • After a deletion request: our support team deletes all of the above within 30 days, as described on the deletion page.
  • Backups: Backup copies of deleted data may remain for up to 30 days, then are deleted automatically.

11. Your rights

  • Access and export: your data is visible in the app; reports and statements export to CSV and PDF.
  • Correction: in the app, or by contacting us.
  • Deletion: all or part — see Account and data deletion.

If you are a customer or employee of a business using the app

The business enters your data and is responsible for it; we store it on its behalf. Please ask the business first to access, correct or delete it; if that fails, contact us and we will reach out to them.

12. Children

The app is intended for business owners, not children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13, and we delete such data when we learn of it.

13. Changes

We update this policy whenever what the app does with data changes; the date at the top shows the latest update. Material changes are announced inside the app.

14. Contact

For any privacy question: hypercare.rivora@gmail.com. See also the Terms of Use.
